Privacy Policy

rclone-gdrive is a personal tool used only by its owner to sync their own Google Drive files.

  • Google Drive data is accessed only on the owner's own computer, by rclone.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only in the local rclone config file and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
